## Key Findings

- **Female students outnumber males**: In 2025, there were 99,483 female students compared to 62,408 male students in higher education institutions.
- **Gender gap widening**: The female-to-male ratio has increased from 1.48 in 2010 to 1.59 in 2025.
- **Peak enrollment**: Both sexes saw peak enrollment around 2012-2014, with a gradual decline afterward.
- **Recent recovery**: Student numbers have been recovering since 2020, especially among females.

## Data Coverage

This dataset includes students enrolled in:
- Universities
- Institutes
- Colleges

Data is available by:
- **Sex**: Male and Female
- **Region**: All 21 aimags, the Capital (Ulaanbaatar), and 5 regional aggregates
- **Year**: 2010-2025 (16 years)
