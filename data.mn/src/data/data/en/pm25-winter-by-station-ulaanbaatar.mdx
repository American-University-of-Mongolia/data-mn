Mean PM2.5 (µg/m³) from December 2025 to February 2026 at 17 Ulaanbaatar monitoring stations with 60 or more valid days. Bayankhoshuu-6 had the highest winter mean at 132.6, followed by Dambadarjaa-5 (108.0) and Khailaast (99.9); Misheel Expo had the lowest at 20.5. Stations with fewer than 60 valid days are left out.

Each bar is the mean of a station's valid daily PM2.5 values from December 2025 to February 2026. Only stations with 60 or more valid days are shown; the others were left out for too few days. The dashed line marks the 24-hour PM2.5 limit of 50 µg/m³ in the Mongolian standard (MNS 4585:2016).

## How this was cleaned

Daily PM2.5 station values come from [agaar.gov.mn](https://agaar.gov.mn) (NAMEM). A station-day was dropped if PM2.5 was negative, if PM2.5 exceeded 1.1 times PM10 on the same day (and 50), if it was more than 5 times that day's median across all stations (and above 150), or if the same value repeated on 5 or more consecutive days at one station. In all, 163 of 16,115 station-days to 6 October 2026 were dropped. Related: [Air Pollution Concentration in Ulaanbaatar (SO₂)](/en/data/air-pollution-concentration).
