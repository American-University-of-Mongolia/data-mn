<div role="note" className="mb-4 text-sm text-slate-600 dark:text-slate-400"><strong>Methodology.</strong> Each monthly value is a simple average of the NSO "Average" SO₂ concentration across the Ulaanbaatar stations that have data that month. Stations without data are left out, and no values are estimated or interpolated. The 17 stations NSO lists did not all operate for the whole period: 2–4 stations have data each month in 2002–2007 and 8–14 from 2015 onward, and no month has all 17. Only three stations (13th micro district, 1st micro district and West crossroad) have data from 2002 onward. Part of any change over time can therefore come from which stations were reporting. For trend analysis, use the station-level NSO table "SULPHUR DIOXIDE, by location, by month", which also gives each station's number of measurements.</div>
