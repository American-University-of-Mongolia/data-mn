## Key Findings

- **Total offenders in 2024**: 25,291 individuals convicted of crimes
- **Most common crime**: Property crimes (53% of all offenders in 2024)
- **Second most common**: Crimes against health (27% of offenders)
- **Traffic violations**: 2,718 offenders (11% of total)
