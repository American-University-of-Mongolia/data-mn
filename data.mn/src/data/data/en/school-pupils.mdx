## Key Findings

- **Total pupils in 2025**: 819,907 students enrolled in general educational schools
- **Steady growth**: Total enrollment increased by 48% from 551,953 (2016) to 819,907 (2025)
- **Primary level dominates**: 378,408 pupils in primary schools (46% of total)
- **Gender balance**: 407,097 female students (49.7% of total)
- **Public schools**: 739,382 pupils (90.2%) attend public schools

## Data Breakdown

The dataset includes 7 indicators:
- **Total**: All pupils across all levels
- **Of which: Female**: Female pupil count
- **Primary**: Primary school level (Grades 1-5)
- **Secondary**: Secondary school level (Grades 6-9)
- **High**: High school level (Grades 10-12)
- **Public**: Government-owned schools
- **Private**: Privately-owned schools
