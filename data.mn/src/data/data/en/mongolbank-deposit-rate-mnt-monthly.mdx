Monthly weighted average interest rate on MNT-denominated deposits in Mongolia's banking system, published by the Bank of Mongolia via its interest rate statistics portal. Data coverage is quarterly from December 2008 to December 2010, and monthly from January 2011 onward. This dataset covers MNT deposits only; see `mongolbank-reference-rates` for a multi-rate comparison including FX deposit rates sourced from the statistics API.
