Monthly foreign trade statistics for Mongolia from January 1997 to September 2026, covering total turnover, exports, imports, and trade balance in million USD.

Monthly data reveals strong seasonality in Mongolia's trade flows — exports spike during summer and autumn coal shipping seasons, while imports rise mid-year alongside construction material deliveries. In September 2026, turnover was 3,163.39 million USD, with exports of 1,804.07 million USD, imports of 1,359.31 million USD and a trade surplus of 444.76 million USD.
