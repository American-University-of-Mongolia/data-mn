Mongolia's Consumer Price Index broken down across 13 spending categories (base year 2020=100), covering January 2010 through March 2026. Key story angles:

- **Food inflation** (food and non-alcoholic beverages) has repeatedly outpaced headline CPI, driven by import dependence and seasonal supply shocks.
- **Housing, water, electricity and fuels** reflects energy policy changes and urbanization pressures in Ulaanbaatar.
- **Transport** tracks fuel price volatility and mirrors global oil cycles.
- **Communication** is the only category showing a sustained long-run decline, reflecting competition and falling device costs.

For the headline CPI trend, see [Mongolia National Consumer Price Index, Monthly](/en/data/cpi-national-monthly).

For Ulaanbaatar-specific monthly inflation, see [Monthly Inflation in Ulaanbaatar](/en/data/cpi-monthly-ulaanbaatar).
