Ulaanbaatar's PM2.5 daily average exceeded Mongolia's 50 µg/m³ limit on 77 days between October 2025 and September 2026, all between November 2025 and February 2026; December 2025 had 27 of 31 days above the limit, and the worst city-wide day averaged 112.1 µg/m³ in February 2026.

A day counts as above the limit when the average of all reporting Ulaanbaatar monitoring stations (at least 8 stations) is above 50 µg/m³, the 24-hour PM2.5 limit in Mongolian standard MNS 4585:2016. The chart shows complete months from October 2025; the tooltip lists the days with data and the highest city-wide daily mean in each month.

**How this was cleaned.** Station-day values were dropped when negative, when PM2.5 was more than 1.1 times same-day PM10 and above 50, when PM2.5 was above 5 times that day's median across all stations and above 150, or when a station repeated the same value on 5 or more consecutive days. This removed 163 of 16,115 station-days (data to 2026-10-06). Source: [agaar.gov.mn](https://agaar.gov.mn), National Agency for Meteorology and Environmental Monitoring (NAMEM).
