<aside aria-label="Coverage and limitations" className="mb-6 rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700 p-4 text-sm">
<p><strong>Coverage and limitations</strong></p>
<p>The 2022–23 and 2023–24 seasons contain results only, with no player box scores. The 2024–25 collection has 172 recorded games: 167 have box scores and five have only results. Its reports and result posters were transcribed from images. The 2025–26 collection has 175 recorded games with box scores: 135 regular-season games from bilingual league pages, plus 37 playoff and three play-in games transcribed from images. These counts describe the recorded collection, not a claim that every season is complete.</p>
<p>Missing source fields stay empty, not zero. Review flags identify games with documented source inconsistencies; a flag does not necessarily indicate a scoring discrepancy. All 684 supplied team box scores reconcile player points with team totals and recorded final scores. English names use the league spelling where available; other names are transliterations and are not verified official spellings.</p>
<p>Exclude did-not-play rows from appearance counts and player averages.</p>
