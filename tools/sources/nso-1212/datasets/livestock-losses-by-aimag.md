# livestock-losses-by-aimag

**Parent:** `nso-livestock-losses`
**Source table:** `DT_NSO_1001_029V1.px`
**Selection:** Total animals (`0`), the 21 aimags plus capital (`511`), annual 1971–2025.
**Measure:** Adult livestock losses, thousand head.
**CSV:** `year,region,value` / `он,бүс,утга`, 1,210 rows per language.
**Charts:** Linked choropleth and top-six ranking, controlled by one 1971–2025 year slider. Missing map values are grey; colour bounds stay constant between years.
**Downloads:** All regions and years in English/Mongolian CSVs and matching single-language wide XLSX files.

See [parent definition](nso-livestock-losses.md) for source endpoints, definitions, exclusions, reproduction, update policy and audit evidence.
