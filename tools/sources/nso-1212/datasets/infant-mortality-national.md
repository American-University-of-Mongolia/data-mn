# Infant Mortality Rate - National

## Dataset Information

**ID**: `infant-mortality-national`
**Parent**: `nso-infant-mortality`
**Type**: Split Dataset
**Source**: NSO 1212.mn
**Table ID**: `DT_NSO_2001_014V1.px`

## Description

Mongolia's national infant mortality rate from 2000 to 2024, showing deaths of infants under 1 year of age per 1,000 live births. This dataset tracks progress in child health outcomes.

## Data Structure

### Output
- **year**: 2000-2024
- **value**: Infant mortality rate per 1,000 live births

## Data Characteristics

- **Temporal Coverage**: 2000-2024
- **Geographic Coverage**: National
- **Frequency**: Annual
- **Unit**: Deaths per 1,000 live births
