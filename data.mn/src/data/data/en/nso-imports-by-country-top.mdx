Annual imports to Mongolia from the top 10 trading partner countries from 2005 to 2025, in USD thousands.

Mongolia's imports are more geographically diversified than its exports. Russia supplies the majority of petroleum products, making fuel supply a key dependency. China provides manufactured goods, electronics, and construction materials. Japan, South Korea, and Germany contribute machinery and vehicles. This diversification provides some resilience compared to the heavy export concentration on China.
