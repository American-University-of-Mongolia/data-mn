# livestock-total-by-aimag

**Parent:** `nso-livestock-by-aimag`
**Source table:** `DT_NSO_1001_109V1.px`
**Selection:** `livestock_type = Total` (MN: Бүгд), `region_level = aimag` (21 aimags plus Ulaanbaatar city; the Ulaanbaatar regional group is excluded), annual 1970–2025.
**Measure:** Number of livestock, thousand head.
**CSV:** `year,region,value` / `он,бүс,утга`, 1,232 rows per language (22 units x 56 years); blank = missing, never 0.
**Charts:** Linked choropleth and top-six ranking, controlled by one 1970–2025 year slider (default 2025). Missing map values are grey; colour bounds stay constant between years.
**Downloads:** All regions and years in English/Mongolian CSVs and matching single-language wide XLSX files.
**Known gaps:** Govisumber 1970–1990; Orkhon total 1970–1975 and 1979; Darkhan-Uul and Ulaanbaatar total 1979.

See [parent definition](nso-livestock-by-aimag.md) for source endpoints, definitions, exclusions and quirks.
